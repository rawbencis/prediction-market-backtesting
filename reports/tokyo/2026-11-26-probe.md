**Where it differs from the brief's letter (deviations):**
- **Interpreter**: venv instead of `/usr/bin/python3` (§1.7).
- **Paper records kept as is**: the brief asks for "the same record kinds and ids as the paper trader". The probe logs all of them unchanged, including the paper's `hedge` and `settle` (the paper *model*'s hedge and settle, now useful as a per-trigger reference); the probe's real execution uses new kinds (`l_order`, `l_fill`, `pm_order`, `pm_fill`, `pair`, `unwind`, `unhedged`, `probe_settle`, …).
- **Clip sizes are whole shares** (rounded up): fits both size steps and keeps Polymarket BUY amounts at ≤ 2 decimals (the maker-amount precision rejection Octopus hit in #26). A Lighter fill or a PM partial that leaves < 1 share, or a remainder below both venues' 5-share minimum, cannot be hedged or unwound: it is **held to settlement** and logged (`unhedged.residual_held`).
- **Loss cap counts a pair's locked P&L at hedge time** (plus unwind losses at once, and the settlement residual), so a run of losing pairs stops the probe the same day rather than ~15 min later.
- **Dry logs go to `~/lp-arb/probe-dry`** and carry `mode: dry`; the live ledger ignores them. Mixing would make the live reconcile see fake positions.
- **`API_KEY_INDEX` and `SIGNATURE_TYPE` are not redacted** (1–3 digit selectors; redacting "2" corrupts every number). They are never logged deliberately. Everything else from SSM, plus the derived PM API key/secret/passphrase, is.
- **The probe refuses live mode if reused code changed** (sha256 pins on 6 Stat_Arb files and rd3_live.py) — an addition, not in the brief.
- **Settlement after a restart**: windows that ended while the probe was down are settled from Gamma + Lighter's REST `outcome`, so their open cost does not stay reserved. Addition.

### Stale-book guard (`lp_arb/probe.py` only)
- **What it measures.** At the trigger (in the `trigger` log hook) and again at +250 ms, per venue:
  - `age_ms` = local now − exchange timestamp of the book's last update;
  - `lag_ms` = local receive time of that update − its exchange timestamp;
  - `since_recv_ms`.
- **Limits.** Polymarket > 300 ms on either measure → `skip` `stale_pm`. Lighter > 100 ms → `stale_lighter`. No exchange timestamp yet counts as stale. Staleness is checked before the 4c edge.
- **Logging.** Every `persist` record carries the ages at both moments. The skip says where it went stale (`at`).
- **Definitions.** The Polymarket book is the paper's single book per window: the Up token's, with side a's Down hedge priced at 1 − Up bid. `age_ms` ≥ `lag_ms` by construction.
- **Tests.** 8 new (55 in `test_probe.py`): each venue and each measure at the trigger and at +250 ms, both limits exactly (pass), and a missing timestamp (stale).

### `--mode check`
- **Loading.** It loads SSM and builds both clients through the same `build_live` as live mode: pins verified first, every value redacted, stdout/stderr scrubbed. That authenticates both venues: the Lighter signer's `check_client` plus authenticated reads; Polymarket L1 → derived L2 credentials plus L2 reads.
- **Output.** One JSON document; exit 0 if ok, 1 with `problems`. It contains:
  - Lighter account `account_type`, `account_trading_mode`, `status` (raw codes), `user_tier_name`, fee ticks, balance, collateral, positions (including `bo_positions`) and open orders;
  - the current BTC 15m market with its `status` / `market_type` (public data today: `active`, `binary_options`);
  - whether the signer client registers that market, and `verdict` (`yes_by_api` / `no`);
  - the Polymarket client version and CLOB `/version`;
  - cash, the USDC allowance to the exchange a BUY is signed for (from the client's own order builder: v2 → `exchange_v2`, v3 → `exchange_v3`), all raw allowances and `closed_only`;
  - Polymarket open orders and positions;
  - the reconcile that a live start would run.
- **Not provable without an order:** Lighter has no per-account binary permission endpoint, so "tradable for this account" stays an API-side verdict until the first order. The output says so.
- **Tests (fakes only):**
  - everything read, nothing placed (the order methods raise);
  - every problem flagged (inactive market, low balance, allowance $12, closed-only, foreign position or order);
  - a venue error doesn't abort the check;
  - `main --mode check` loads secrets exactly once through the live path, writes no log, and never prints a planted key or funder, even when a fake venue echoes the key;
  - a pin mismatch refuses before any secret is read;
  - the spender mapping matches `py_clob_client_v2`'s config.

### `lp_arb/probe_vs_paper.py --day YYYY-MM-DD`
- **Join.** By trigger id, then (window, side, ≤ 250 ms) for ids that differ only by the separate sockets' millisecond. Both sides are limited to the probe's uptime, including a day file that starts mid-run.
- **Reports:**
  - triggers in both (exact / by time), probe-only and paper-only;
  - skips by reason;
  - Lighter IOC fill rate live vs paper d500 on the same triggers, plus a confusion table;
  - per pair: live vs paper c/share, Lighter slippage vs the trigger price, PM slippage vs the trigger price and vs the ask at send, fees modelled vs charged;
