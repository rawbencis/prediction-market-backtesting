### T2. Two legs that never communicate (done 09:57 UTC)

**Setup.** Code: `overnight/t2_legs.py`. The grid was written to `frozen/t2_grid.json` and committed (8b4ac18) before the dev run: 84 cells (A 48, B 16, C 16, D 4) × Q ∈ {5, 50} shares. Leg P uses the PM `live` scenario in PM exchange time: seen +9 ms; post +55 ms, or +190 ms if the best moved in the last 1 s; cancel +90; taker hold 150 ms; strict maker fills. Leg L runs in Lighter exchange time: seen +3, post 0, taker hold 300 ms (140 also recorded), cancel 300. Its maker fills come only from recorded Lighter prints strictly through our price. Every position is "long Up or Down at cost c", settled on its own venue's outcome. Lighter vs PM settlement agrees on 2,097/2,097 windows (T1). PM prints were recorded only from 11-13 21h, so maker fills are impossible before that. **Dev = the 774 BTC+ETH windows of 11-13 21h → 11-17 that have PM prints.** 11-11..12 are excluded.

**Structural point.** The legs never exchange information, so a window's P&L is exactly P&L(P) + P&L(L), and its expectation is the sum of the two legs' expectations. Pairing can only change variance and unhedged exposure, not edge. The placebo re-pairs L legs with random other windows of the same asset (20 draws). It answers "do fills co-occur more than chance?" and "does pairing reduce risk?"

**What this says:**
1. **The Lighter leg is where the money goes.** As a resting maker, strict fills make it adversely selected: −8 to −12¢/share, with the 1 s markout already −8 to −11¢. As a taker it pays Lighter's wide spread, −3 to −5¢ at 1 s. Lighter quotes are mostly 712772's (T1), and they are not stale on Lighter's own evidence. Staleness is only visible against Polymarket, which is exactly the information T2 forbids.
2. **The legs do not hedge each other in expectation.** Both-filled counts equal the placebo's (B 8,310 vs 8,276; C 5,413 vs 5,324). In A they are below the placebo (12,018 vs 13,123), so the legs fill together *less* than chance. That's because a move that fills P's Up bid is a move that pulls Lighter's price away from L's Down bid. Pairing does halve the window P&L sd (1.2–1.6 vs 2.5–3.2), so it hedges outcome risk, but on a negative mean.
3. **D (partner guessing):** P's inference "Lighter filled iff PM printed through Lighter's price" is **wrong 56–75% of the time**; L's mirror inference is wrong 12–18%. Each wrong "partner filled" guess makes the leg cross the spread to complete a pair that doesn't exist. Pair cost then goes to 1.06, and every D cell loses 9–11¢/share.
