## 1. Verdict

1. **V0 fails with the brief's latencies and passes once, as amended (operator decision B).** The amendment adds a Polymarket competitor term and uses measured latencies only: Polymarket match = London arrival + 320 ms, Lighter match = send + 161 ms.
   - 11-26: sim −1.28 vs live −2.69 ¢/sh, gap **1.41** (limit 1.5).
   - 11-27: sim −1.60 vs live −0.93 ¢/sh, gap 0.67.
   - Median PM slippage: 9.0 vs 9.0 and 8.0 vs 7.5 ¢.
   - The pass on 11-26 is narrow. The sim is still too optimistic on the Polymarket leg: it pairs 61 vs 48 live, unwinds 1 vs 10 live, and averages 1.4 vs 1.75 attempts per pair.
2. **The dev pick** (frozen rule: Lighter view lag 120 ms, ≥ 20 attempts/day, highest 90% lower bound) is **threshold 6 ¢, L 0, timing (a), M 1 ¢**.
   - Dev result: **+1.36 ¢ per attempted share, $3.68/day at 5 shares, 90% CI [+0.16, +8.52]**, 54 attempts/day, 7 of 10 days positive.
   - It is the only lag-120 cell with a positive lower bound.
   - Pushed in `frozen/PF1_pick.json` (commit f4658ec) before any validation day was run.
3. **The dev result is mostly an artefact of gaps in Dublin's Polymarket feed.** The feed has silent stalls: no book rows, no trades, no drop marker, for up to minutes.
   - During a stall the simulator keeps matching against the last book it saw, while the real market has moved.
   - 48 of the pick's 540 dev attempts (9%) matched on a book older than 5 s. They carry **$25.21 of the $36.81** dev P&L.
   - One window (11-20 06:00 UTC, a 176 s stall) alone gives $23.51.
   - Excluding attempts on books older than 5 s, the pick makes **+0.47 ¢/sh, $1.16/day**. With a 2 s cutoff it is +0.40 ¢/sh.
4. **Validation (11-25..27, descriptive): +0.41 ¢/sh, $1.20/day, 90% CI [−1.19, +3.59]**.
   - 175 attempts; PM fill rate 31%, Lighter fill rate 36%.
   - Positive on 2 of 3 days; 11-27 is a partial day, to about 09:20 UTC.
   - Without books older than 5 s: +0.38 ¢/sh.
5. **Reading.** On clean books, PF1 at the pick earns roughly +0.4–0.5 ¢ per attempted share. That is well under the holdout bar of **+2.0 ¢/sh**, and the dev lower bound is only positive because of stale books. **I expect the holdout to fail.** The pick stays frozen and is read once on/after 12-05 as briefed.
6. **Decision before 12-05.** The holdout days will contain the same kind of feed stalls, and the frozen model would score them the same way. Two options:
   - Add a stale-book guard (for example, no Polymarket fill when the book in force is more than 2 s old). This is a correction to the data handling, but it comes after seeing dev, so it would need to be registered as an amendment and the pick re-derived.
   - Keep the pick as is and read the holdout with the age diagnostic alongside.

## 2. Amendment (operator decision B, commit 006df9a, pushed before any dev run)

**Polymarket competitor term** (`engine.pm_fill`).
- At our match, the shares the Dublin trade feed shows other takers took at our limit or better, between our arrival and our match, are removed from the best levels first.
- Trades counted for a buy of our token: taker BUY of the same token at ≤ limit, and taker SELL of the other token at 1 − p ≤ limit (that hits the mirrored book). A sell counts the reverse.
- It applies to every Polymarket fill, including unwind sells.
- The feed has one row per trade, with the taker side. Our own live probe fills appear in it: for example a Down BUY of 5.277778 at 0.54, 453 ms after our send. In V0 those are marked and excluded: 262 / 181 shares on 11-26 / 27.

**Latencies (measured, not fitted).**
- Polymarket: match = arrival in London + 320 ms. That is live ack p50 ≈ 435 ms minus ≈ 115 ms Tokyo → London. Sensitivity rows: 250 and 400 ms.
- Lighter: match = arrival + 158 ms (send → exchange tx 161 ms from Tokyo, minus ~3 ms Tokyo → Lighter). V0 uses send + 161 ms.

**Unchanged:** triggers, the 72-cell grid, views, unwind rules, fees, the pick rule, the stop-early rule, the splits and the holdout pass rules.

**How much size the competitor term removes in V0:**

| day | PM orders | orders with competitor trades | shares removed | displayed size the book itself lost between arrival and match | fill lost to the term | orders zeroed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 11-26 | 88 | 29 | 2,048 | 14,566 | 10 sh | 2 |
| 11-27 | 69 | 27 | 1,087 | 5,346 | 5 sh | 1 |

- The term removes a lot of size, but at a 5-share clip it rarely binds. The book in force at a 320 ms-later match has usually already lost far more.
- The V0 improvement over the spec run comes almost entirely from the slower Polymarket match. Where both apply, the term is pessimistic: some removed trades are also reflected in the book at match.

## 11. Deviations and notes

- **Competitor term on unwind sells:** the operator's term is applied to every Polymarket fill, unwind sells included, as part of "the fill model".
- **Lighter timing from Dublin:** the measured 161 ms is Tokyo send → exchange tx, so from Dublin the Lighter leg matches at send + 100 ms (one way) + 158 ms.
- **`pf1/agg.py` bug:** `A.clip` resolved to `DataFrame.clip`, so the first aggregation returned an empty table. I fixed it to `A["clip"]`, with no model change, in the pick commit, and the table was produced only after the fix.
- **Memory:**
  - V0 holds two days of windows in one process and peaked at 1.13 GB (under the 1.2 GB cap).
  - The day runner (`pf1/run_days.sh`: one day per process, nice 15, MemoryMax 1200M, waits while less than 800 MB is available) peaked at 280–415 MB per day. Available memory was ≥ 1.75 GB at every day start.
  - Earlier in the session, the first extraction attempt drove available memory to ~120 MB for about 2 minutes before I killed it. The recorders kept running.
- **Lighter loader:** it now reads in chunks and keeps only the wanted windows. V0 output is identical.
- **V2 on the validation days** was produced by the runner but not examined. The brief asks for V2 on dev only.
