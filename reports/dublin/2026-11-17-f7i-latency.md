## 2. Exploratory grid under live_lean (dev, then the best 2 once on 11-16)

Grid design:
- **Configs:** entry {new best level (F7i's), stable 1 s} × offset {+1, 0, −1, −2 ticks from the best bid of the token bought} × pulls {alt, alt+out, none} × TTL {5 s, 30 s, until 20 s before close} × pair cap {off, 0.99} = **144 configs** (`f8_grid.py`).
- **Simulation:** both sides are simulated in time order with one live order per side. The cap skips a post when (average price of the other token already held + our price) > 0.99. Cancels always wait for the ack (`ackc`).
- **Grid models:** dsim fills and pess queue only. Strict fills were used only for the holdout picks.
- **Selection rule** (`frozen/F8_rule.json`, committed before any grid output existed):
  - Eligible: dev fills/day ≥ 200 and dev 1 s markout > 0.
  - Rank by the lower bound of the dev settle CI, and take the top 2.
- **Picks:** `frozen/F8.json`, committed in `7c051c0` before the holdout run.
