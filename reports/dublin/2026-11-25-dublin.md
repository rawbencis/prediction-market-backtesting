## 1. D1: taker fee history for Tokyo

**File:** `reports/dublin/fees_by_window.csv` (25,830 rows): `asset,tf,window_start_utc,fee_rate,source,n_taker_rows`.
- **Scope:** BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and DOGE × 5m and 15m, every window from 2026-11-11 00:00 to the last window that ended before the census stopped (BTC 5m: 11-24 10:55).
- **`fee_rate`:** the median over the window's taker rows of fee ÷ (size·p·(1−p)).
  - Taker rows are the rows with fee > 0; maker rows carry −1.
  - Rows with p outside 0.005–0.995 are dropped.
  - The window is the market's slug epoch, which is the window start.
- **`source`:** `census`, or `none` with an empty rate when a window has no taker rows. `none` covers:
  - every window before the census started (**11-11 00:00 → 11-14 ~21:40**);
  - quiet alt windows;
  - nothing after 11-24 10:55, because the file stops there.
- **Wallet `0xc387c2a4` rows dated 11-24 are dropped** before anything is computed (frozen watch).
- **Code:**

### 3.2 Findings for Tokyo (T4)

- **The 1h/4h series do NOT settle on the window's average.**
  - Rule: Up iff TWAP60(end) ≥ TWAP60(start), where TWAP60(t) is the Chainlink 60 s TWAP stream value stamped at t.

  - The market text ("TWAP … of the time range") is misleading. A model that treats the window average as the settlement value is wrong.
