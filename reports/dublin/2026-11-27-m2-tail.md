## M2-0. The wallet's own late fills

### Method (recorded days, `m2/m0a_fills.py`, one day per capped job)

- **Fills and timing.**
  - Fills come from the census rows of wallet `0x1292…a5a6` (BTC 5m), with ≥ 240 s elapsed at the **match time**.
  - The match time is the exchange stamp of the print with the same tx hash. 1,277 of 1,282 fills joined; the rest use `trade_ts`.
  - All 1,282 late fills are buys: 841 taker, 441 maker.
- **Stake and payout.** Stake is price × size + the census fee (taker only). Payout is size if the side won.
- **FV.** The `d2_samples.py --final` formula: Chainlink stream as S, `twap60` at the start second as K. It is copied into `fv_up()`.
- **Strike gap.** 1e4·(K − Chainlink stamped at the start second)/Chainlink, signed so that **positive favours the side bought**.
- **Level age** (deviation from a naive reading, see Deviations). The print's exchange stamp trails the book changes of its own sweep by a median 32 ms (p90 65 ms). In the last book row before the print, the ask has therefore usually already moved past the fill price. The age is the length of the run of side-ask ≤ fill price that ends at the last such row within 250 ms before the print.
- **Intervals.** 90% percentile bootstrap over windows, 4,000 resamples. "Ex top-3" drops the 3 fills with the largest profit in that subset.
- **Timing check for 0b.** The Data API `timestamp` minus the recorded match time is p5/p50/p95 = 0.3 / 1.9 / 3.2 s (1,277 fills). The brief's "block time − 3 s" therefore puts the match about 1 s *early* at the median, so the lifetime 240 s cut is slightly loose.
  - **Cross-check.** On 11-15..27 the lifetime set (API, ts − 3) and the recorded set (census, print time) give the same ROI: all late buys +3.1% vs +3.1%, tail +13.2% vs +12.4%.

### What distinguishes the late tail buys (descriptive)

- **They chase a rising underdog.** In 92% of tail taker buys, the side's ask 1 s before the match was at or below the price OhioAI paid. The fill price was a median **+6.0¢** above that ask (p25/p75 +2.3 / +9.9¢). OhioAI buys the tail token *after* it has started to reprice, as part of a burst. Every tail taker buy had another taker on the same token within ±1 s, with a mean of 19.6 taker rows and about 1,060 shares.
- **The levels it takes had mostly been resting.** 80% of the levels had stood ≥ 500 ms and 69% ≥ 2 s. Afterwards, the ask was still at or below the fill price in 45% of cases at +500 ms and 52% at +2 s, so roughly half of the time size remained.
- **Seconds left matter most.**
  - 60–40 s left: tail +48.5% [+4, +96]; ex top-3 +13.6% [−17, +47].
  - 40–20 s left: −28%; ex top-3 −83%.
  - 20–5 s left: −32%; ex top-3 −100%.
  - The same pattern holds lifetime: 60–40 s ex top-3 +18% [−2, +40]; 20–5 s −88%.
- **Strike gap and side** separate nothing beyond noise (Table 3).
- **Stake size: the dossier is not reproduced.**
  - The tail stake per window rose: median $6 in September, $15 in October, $25 in November.
  - The median per *fill* on the recorded days is $10.27.
  - There was no late buying at all before September.

### Stop rule (fixed in the brief)

M2 closes only if both conditions hold:

1. **Lifetime tail ex top-3 interval includes 0 or is below it.** It is +38.5% [+8.6, +72.5]: **fails**.
2. **In Table 2 the price is at least as close to the win rate as the FV in every bucket.** The FV is closer in the 0–5¢ bucket (16 fills) and the > 15¢ bucket (8 fills): **fails**. Both are small buckets. In the bucket holding 87% of the fills (FV < price), the price is far closer: 4.1 vs 12.2¢.

## M2-1. Tail side of the final minute (dev 11-14..11-23; all ten days are dev)

### Method

- **Sampler.** `m2/m2_samples.py` is `dublin1124/d2_samples.py --final` with two changes only:
  - The match and pessimistic delays come from env, for the 330/430 ms rows.
  - A strike-gap column is added.
- **Samples.** 1 s samples from 60 to 5 s left, kept only where `fvcl` (Chainlink S, `twap60` K) is finite: 89–90% of samples.
- **Eligibility.** Side ask ≤ 30¢ with ≥ 5 shares.
- **Cells.** g = FV − ask − fee·ask·(1 − ask). Fee 0.05 for BTC 5m and 15m, 0.01 for ETH 5m.
- **Orders.** One FAK per window per side per cell at its first sample. It is matched at t + 205 ms against the standing top of book; the pessimistic fill also needs size at t + 305 ms.
- **Statistics.** These are `d2_cells.stats`: the SE is by window, floored at the binomial SE.
- **Freeze rule.** ≥ 30 filled windows, t ≥ 2.5, both halves > 0, pessimistic > 0, at most 2 cells.

## Deviations from the brief

1. **Level age definition (Table 4).** "At or below its price before the match" is measured on book rows up to the last row with ask ≤ price within 250 ms before the print stamp, not the last row before the stamp. The print stamp trails its own sweep's book changes by about 30 ms. The naive version said 91% of levels "appeared < 100 ms before", which was an artefact; diagnosis in Method.
2. **Late window (0a).** The 240 s cut uses the print's exchange time, not the census `trade_ts` (block time, which is a median 1.2 s later).
3. **Lifetime match time (0b).** "Block time − 3 s" was used as specified. On recorded fills, the API stamp is a median 1.9 s after the match (p95 3.2 s), so the rule shifts fills about 1 s early. Results match the recorded set on the overlap.
4. **Fees (M2-1).** Flat post-switch fees on all ten days: BTC 0.05 (the brief gave no BTC 5m fee), ETH 0.01. Before 11-21 the real fee was 0.07. That would only lower 11-14..20 edges, and since no cell qualified, the sensitivity row was not needed.
5. **Sampler gate.** The sampler still requires 30 min of Binance spot history per window, inherited from `d2_samples.py`, and it computes Binance-based z/Φ columns. Those are **not used** for any cell, selection or P&L; only `fvcl` (Chainlink/`twap60`) is. The gate only drops windows at recording starts.
6. **`0xc387c2a4`.** Its rows are dropped at load on *all* days, not only 11-24+. This only matters for the "other takers within ±1 s" counts.
7. **`wallet_dissect.py`.** Copied unchanged from `research/wallet-dissect` (`cbe5ea3`) into `m2/` and run through `m2/wd_throttled.py`. The wrapper caps every HTTP call, including the Gamma per-slug fallback, at ≤ 1.8 req/s, because the script defaults to 4 req/s. Output went to `~/m2-out/ohioai` (the existing `~/ohioai` was untouched).
8. **Commit check.** The pre-commit grep matched only the identifier `key` (`key = f"{asset}-updown…"`, `def key(r)`, `KeyError`, `tkeys`). The `secret|private|0x[0-9a-f]{64}` part matched nothing. I judged this a false positive and committed.
