## 1. Verdict: CLOSED at dev (frozen stop rule). Holdout not run.

1. **Every one of the 11,232 dev cells loses under `pess`** (2 markets × 864 configs × 13 side filters). Under `prop` all 11,232 lose too.
   - Best cell: −1.69¢/share. Best 90% lower bound: −3.21¢. Median: −3.99¢ (5m) and −3.38¢ (15m).
   - At the volumes that pass the ≥ 30 rungs/day filter, the best cell is −2.51¢/share, about −$36/day at 5 shares/rung.
2. **The stop rule is met by a wide margin.** No cell has a 30 s markout + 0.5¢ above 0. The best 30 s markout is −0.97¢; most cells are around −2¢.
3. **The take-profit does what the martingale argument says.** It changes the shape, not the mean.
   - TP hit rates are 82–93%.
   - Shares that miss the TP lose −42 to −48¢ each. 99.6% of them are on the losing token, which settles at 0.
   - EV moves by ≤ 0.3¢ across x = 1/2/3/5. The synthetic test V1 confirms the TP adds no EV on a martingale.
4. **The side models add nothing.** Each picks the winner 46–52% of the time; S1 is 0.485, the known P(same as previous).
   - Model minus mirror is never positive with CI > 0, except for the seeded random control R on 15m (in 15% of configs). That shows the noise floor of the comparison.
   - S2 ("follow two in a row") is significantly *worse* than its mirror on 5m: −1.76¢ median, CI < 0 in 99% of configs. Its mirror is still −2.75¢ at best.
5. **Pulls help a little, as prior evidence said, but nowhere near enough.**
   - Cancelling on a Binance ≥ 2 bp/250 ms move against the side lifts the 1 s markout by ~0.2¢ (5m: −1.65 → −1.47¢).
   - Net improves by ~0.1–0.2¢/share.
6. **E2 (taker exit at 30 s left) ≈ E1 (hold to settlement).** By 30 s left the losing token's bid side is usually empty: 96–99% of non-TP shares find no bid.

**V1 note.** On a martingale the TP's apparent spread capture is illusory.
- Measured against the mid just *before* the TP fill, it looks like +0.24 to +0.38¢.
- But half the TP fills happen when the bid moves up through our offer. Against the mid at the fill, capture is ~0 (−0.01¢).
- This matters for reading real data: a high TP hit rate with "positive" capture can still be zero EV.

**V3 5m: why −1.72 and not −1.4.** I regenerated the D5 join-maker (`p2_join.py`, `bid|60000|none`, `live`, strict) on 11-17 and 11-22 and split both by time since window open. The simulator reproduces D5 (11-17: −1.39¢; 11-22: −1.69¢). Two things explain the gap:
- **Timing.** 70% of LT1's 5m fills come within 5 s of posting, i.e. in the first 6 s of the window. D5's own fills there are also worse than its window average:
  - 11-17: first 5 s −1.55¢ vs −1.16¢ at 60–180 s.
  - 11-22: first 5 s −1.69¢ vs −1.29¢ at 60–180 s.
- **Order type.** LT1 joins the opening book's existing best bid; D5 joins freshly formed levels. In the same time bucket on 11-22, LT1 is still ~0.6¢ worse (11-17: within noise).
- **Fill age doesn't explain it:** fills within 60 s of posting average −1.69¢.

So the gap comes from the strategy itself, not a sim bug.
