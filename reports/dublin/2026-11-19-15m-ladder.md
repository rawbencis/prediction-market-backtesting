## 1. Verdict
1. **The old "19% one tick inside" was a clock artefact.** The book row that shows a fill comes a median 20 ms *before* its print. Read in the right order, 0x239e's maker shares are 92% at the best bid, 8% behind it, and 0.2% inside.
2. **Its queue place comes mostly from being first to a new level, by speed.**
   - NEW fills: the level appeared < 1 s before it became best. They are 34% of shares and carry the profit: settle +1.53¢ [+0.25, +2.77].
   - 83% of NEW shares are the bid being *improved* by one tick into a gap that had just opened, a **median 8 ms** after it opened (p90 100 ms).
   - We post in 55–190 ms, so this can't be copied.
3. **Early posting at depth exists but doesn't pay.**
   - DEEP fills: the level was displayed below the best for ≥ 30 s. They are 26% of shares, and the wallet sits near the front (filled after ~8% of the level has printed).
   - Markout is positive (+0.31¢ at 1 s), but settle is −0.65¢ [−1.55, +0.35]: −0.19 on dev, −0.93 on 11-17..18.
   - All "posted ≥ 1 s early" fills (DEEP + OTHER, 66% of shares): +0.21¢ at 1 s, settle +0.01¢ [−0.92, +0.89].
4. **Step 2 was not run.** Its gate is that DEEP is the larger class; it isn't (DEEP 26% vs NEW 34%). No `frozen/L15.json`, no holdout run.
5. **No live probe is proposed.** The profitable part of this wallet's maker flow is an ~8 ms race to improve a freshly opened spread. The copyable part (early resting) has ≈ +0.2¢ markouts and settles at zero.

- **The alignment bug:** the book change caused by a fill comes a median **20 ms before** the print (p10 −41, p90 +10, on both books).

**Early posting at depth, or speed?** Both happen, and they do different jobs.
- **Early posting.** About two-thirds of 0x239e's maker shares fill at a level it had joined at least a second before that level became the best bid. The median lead is 78 s for DEEP and 8 s for OTHER.
  - It is then near the front: only 1–8% of the level shown at that point has printed when it fills, and the levels are thick (median 639 shares for DEEP).
  - That is queue place bought by patience, and in principle copyable.
  - These fills are not adversely selected (+0.2 to +0.3¢ at 1 s, the same on dev and out of sample), but they don't settle in profit (+0.01¢ [−0.92, +0.89]; DEEP alone −0.65¢).
- **Speed.** The settlement profit sits in the NEW third: +1.53¢ [+0.25, +2.77], dev +2.78, out of sample +0.77.
  - Five-sixths of that is the bid stepping up one tick into a spread that has just opened. It happens a median 8 ms after the ask moves up, inside 100 ms 91% of the time.
  - The new level is usually small (≤ 30 shares in 77% of cases, about this wallet's order size). That makes it very likely this wallet's own improving order, though the census can't prove it.
  - That is a speed race. Our live posts land 55 ms (calm) to 190 ms (after a book change) after the trigger.
- **Bottom line:** the copyable half is markout-neutral and settles at zero; the half that makes money is the race.

## 4. Step 2: ladder sim
**Not run.** The brief runs Step 2 only if DEEP is the larger class and its 1 s markout is ≥ 0. The markout condition holds (+0.31¢), but DEEP is 26% of shares against NEW's 34%, and OTHER is 40%. No `frozen/L15.json` was written, and 11-17/11-18 were not used for a ladder.
- **The simulator exists but is unrun:** `ladder.py`, committed in `ab52140` as a draft. It works on the 5-level book:
  - pess and prop queues at the landing price;
  - strict fills;
  - prints moved 60 ms earlier, so a fill isn't counted twice against the queue (Step 0);
  - the full 72-cell grid (k × band × minutes × queue).
- **If you want to test DEEP + OTHER together** (66%, the share that posts ≥ 1 s early), the ladder at k = 1–2 is that test. It needs your call, because it overrides the gate. Expect ≈ +0.2¢ at 1 s at best, by the wallet's own numbers, and settle around zero.

## 5. Live probe
None: the wallet's profitable maker flow (NEW, settle +1.5¢) comes from improving a freshly opened spread within ~8 ms, which our 55–190 ms posts can't reach, and the copyable early-resting part settles at +0.01¢ [−0.92, +0.89].

## 6. Limits
- **Who placed the improving order is inferred, not observed.** Level size ≤ 30 shares in 77% of NEW fills, and the census has no order IDs.
- **DEEP undercounts levels that were > 5 levels deep.** Rows below the shown depth are treated as "present but unseen", and a displayed sighting ≥ 30 s back is required. 39% of DEEP fills were below the 5 shown levels at −30 s.
- **Missing data:** 21 windows overlapping corrupt pmbook files are excluded. 11-14 has census data only from 21:41.
- **Markouts** use the pmbook mid, only while > 30 s remain. They differ slightly from the makers report's top-of-book mids (e.g. 0x239e mo 1 s +0.20 here vs +0.17 there).
