## 3. Rule as simulated (`alt_bid.py`)
- **Trigger:** a book row where the UP bid rises (UP side) or the UP ask falls (DOWN side). The new level shows ≥ 5 shares, ≥ 20 s are left, and UP ask − UP bid ≥ 2¢ on that row.
- **Timing (`live`):** decided at row + 9 ms; on the book +190 ms later (the 55 ms fast path never applies, because the triggering row changed the best). Cancels land +90 ms, never before arrival + 90.
- **Order:** post-only BUY 5 at that side's best bid. Pess queue, strict fills (a print through our price fills at most its own size minus the queue ahead).
- **Rest:** until fully filled, 60 s after arrival, or 10 s before the close.
- **Re-entry:** a side may re-enter 2 s after its order is done (full fill, cancel landed, or reject).
- **Limits:** one live order per side, checked on the state known at the decision time (fills with a time up to then).
  - Side: filled + 5 ≤ 10.
  - Lead: (this side filled + 5) − (other side filled + resting) ≤ 5.
  - Second leg: if the other side holds filled or resting shares, this price + their average price ≤ 0.99.
  - An order that would break a limit is skipped, not resized; 5 shares is the minimum order.
- **Settlement:** held to settlement; winners from Polymarket Gamma.
- **P&L, markouts and rebate:**
  - P&L is per share, share-weighted.
  - Markouts use the token mid at the first fill + 1 s / 5 s.
  - Rebate = 0.014·p·(1−p) per share, shown separately; window P&L excludes it.
- **CIs:**
  - All intervals are 90% bootstraps over markets.
  - "CI day" is over only 3 dev days and is close to meaningless; the holdout has 1 day (n/a).
  - z naive = binomial; z by market = clustered on the window.
- **Price bands** are by the price of the token bought.
- **Daily SD** = √(288 · var(window P&L)). It assumes independent windows; BTC window P&L lag-1 autocorrelation is +0.009.

**Reading:**
- The alts move together with BTC, at about +0.3 to +0.4 correlation. Adding one adds about 55–60% to the daily SD; at zero correlation it would add ~40%.
- Live ends with about as many unhedged shares per window as the sim (2.4 vs 2.57), and those drive the variance. So the $ SD figures are probably near live levels or a little low; the mean $/day is not (×0.63 for volume, and the per-share level is too pessimistic). The ratio of BTC + alt to BTC alone is the most portable number.

## 6. What this does not cover
- **Our own orders in the alt books.** The sim's volume and completion biases (section 2) were measured on BTC only. The alt books are thinner (median size at the best when we join: ETH 16, SOL 11, XRP 13, DOGE 6, vs BTC 25), so queue effects may differ there.
- **Holdout size.** One holdout day per alt (288 windows) can't separate ±1¢/share. The pooled 4-day paired difference is the better-powered figure, but dev and holdout point in opposite directions for ETH, SOL and XRP.
- **Live-scaled swing** is an approximation (see the reading above), not a simulation of the live fill rate.
