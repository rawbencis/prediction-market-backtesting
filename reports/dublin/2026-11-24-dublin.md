### D2. Level map on November, and the final minute

**Shared grid, kept as defined.** Dublin-specific choices:
- **Unit:** one settled window; samples every 5 s from open + 10 s to close − 10 s.
- **S:** Binance spot as received here (last trade with local receipt ≤ t).
- **K:** the Chainlink 60 s TWAP at the start second from `feeds-rec/twap60` (nearest within ±2 s) for 82–83% of windows; otherwise the mean of S over the 60 s before the start. The two subsets are reported separately.
- **σ:** σ_l and σ_s from 1 s log returns of the per-second S (30 min and 60 s).
- **τ ≥ 60:** τ_eff = τ − 40.
- **τ < 60:** S is replaced by ((60−τ)R + τS)/60 and sd = σ_l·√(τ³/10800).
- **z, r:** z = ln(S/K) / sd, with sd = σ_l·√τ_eff (or the τ < 60 form above), signed toward the side; r = σ_s/σ_l.
- **Trade:** a taker BUY of the side, one per window per side per cell (the first sample in the cell).
  - It is a FAK at the ask seen, matched at exchange time t + 205 ms (9 + 55 + 150 − 9 receive offset) against the standing top of book, with ≥ 5 shown.
  - Pessimistic fill: ≥ 5 shares still at the paid price at t + 305 ms.
  - Fee 0.05·p(1−p) on BTC, 0.01 on ETH.
- **SE:** clustered by window, and floored at the binomial SE at the paid price, √(p(1−p)/n). The floor was added after the freeze; see below.

**Reading D4.**
- **Order of arrival:**
  - Binance and Coinbase arrive essentially together. Binance is first in 55–74% of matched moves, median lag 10–39 ms.
  - Deribit's perp ticker trails both by a median ~230–310 ms; the 100 ms channel is batched.
  - Chainlink is not a fast feed: 9–25 moves of ≥ 2 bp a day, and it trails the exchanges by 0.2–0.9 s.
- **Lead over the book:**
  - A Binance or Coinbase move of ≥ 2 bp is followed by a BTC 5m reprice 90–92% of the time.
  - The median lead is ~200–290 ms on 5m and ~130–145 ms on 15m (the 15m book is faster, as in the 11-18 report).
  - The share of those reprices that come more than 190 ms after the exchange move (our post latency) is 51–66% on 5m and 39–41% on 15m. More than 90 ms (our cancel): 70–81% on 5m, 60–64% on 15m.
- **Does any feed give a maker here time to cancel?**
  - **For the moves it sees, yes:** on 5m, roughly three times in four a cancel sent on a Coinbase or Binance print would land before the book reprices.
  - **But exchange moves explain only a small part of the book's moves.** Only 13–17% of ≥ 1¢ book moves have a same-sign ≥ 2 bp Coinbase or Deribit move in the 2 s before them (Binance: 9–13%). Only 11–14% have one more than 90 ms earlier.
  - So a feed-driven cancel can protect against at most about one adverse book move in seven. That fits the MM ablation, where the stale-quote shield on Binance at 100 ms recovered +0.08¢/share and +0.5¢ only in combination.
- **Coinbase adds nothing over Binance from Dublin:** same timing, same coverage. Deribit is later than both.
- The cancel-race arithmetic can't make the maker's −1¢ markout positive. Most of the adverse flow arrives without any exchange move this box can see first.
