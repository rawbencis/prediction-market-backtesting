**What the cuts show (239e726f1f…):**
- **At / behind / inside the bid:**
  - 69% of its maker shares fill **at the best bid** (+0.33¢ at 1 s).
  - Fills one tick *inside* the spread are adversely selected (−0.64¢ at 1 s), like our improve orders.
  - Fills behind the best bid (an order left resting under a better level) have the best markouts (+1.2 to +3.2¢).
- **Queue:** the best markouts come where its fill is a small slice of a thick level (fill < 10% of the level: +0.56¢ at 1 s; levels ≥ 1,000 shares: +0.40¢). An order that has rested in a large level for a while gets filled by ordinary flow. Our newcomer behind the same displayed size only fills when the level is run through.
- **Price band (selection):** settlement profit is in the tokens it ends up holding at **60–100¢ (+2.2 to +5.4¢/share, +$1,377)**. Its longshot fills **< 10¢ lose −1.5¢/share (−$561)**, despite positive short markouts there. The paired other side mostly expires worthless.
- **Timing / signals:**
  - Fills right after a book change (< 100 ms calm) carry the most settle P&L (+$877) with ~0 markout.
  - Fills after a BTC 15m move of ≥ 1.5¢ toward the token, or an alt 15m move ≥ 0.75¢ toward it, are its best (+6.1 / +2.6¢ settle, +2.0 / +0.9¢ at 1 s), but they are 3–14% of shares.
  - Imbalance at the fill shows no clear pattern.
- **Minutes left:** positive with < 3 minutes left (+2.6 / +1.1¢) and 5–10 minutes (+2.0¢); negative with 10–15 minutes left (−2.0¢).

## 4. Queue position or selection?
**Both, and only the markout part is robust out of sample.**
- **The queue part** is real but small: ≈ +0.2¢/share at 1–5 s on fills at the best bid, positive in-sample (CI > 0) and weaker out of sample (+0.11¢ [−0.02, +0.23]).
  - It comes from being in the queue early, inside thick levels, which a newcomer landing in 55–190 ms behind all displayed size can't copy. Our sim of the newcomer is −1.1¢.
  - That's the main lesson: the recorded book can't hand us their queue place, so the "always at the best bid" style isn't copyable by joining late.
- **The rest of the profit is at settlement**, i.e. selection, mainly *which side ends up as the held position*: favourites at 60–100¢ earn, the unpaired longshot side loses.
  - In-sample that is ~+1¢/share over the markout; out of sample it shrinks to +0.3¢ with a CI spanning 0.
  - The second wallet (e29042f5d9…) lost its settlement edge out of sample entirely.

**If I had to freeze a selection rule** (not tested on 11-17/18, as asked):
- **Name:** `F15-fav-join`, a BTC 15m post-only BUY 5 at the best bid of the token whose best bid is 0.60–0.90.
- **When:** with 1–10 minutes left, only when the level already shows ≥ 200 shares (so that we join a thick level, as the wallet's best fills do).
- **Hold:** rest until 30 s before the close or until filled, no pulls; one order per token per window; hold to settlement.
- **Pass/kill on ≥ 10 fresh days from 2026-11-19:**
  - pass = settle market-CI low > 0 and 1 s markout ≥ 0;
  - kill = CI high < +0.5¢/share or 1 s markout < −0.5¢ on the first 5 days.
- **Expectation:** our own Part 2 sim already shows joining 60–90¢ tokens late gets −1.1 to −1.4¢ at 1 s. So I expect it to fail for the queue reason above, unless the order is placed early enough to sit in front.
